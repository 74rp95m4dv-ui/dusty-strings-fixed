# Dusty Strings Presentation

## Purpose

Every feature should feel premium.

Players should feel like they are living the career of a musician.

Presentation matters as much as mechanics.

---

## Philosophy

Avoid static menus.

Use movement.

Use lighting.

Use transitions.

Use ambience.

Use atmosphere.

---

## UI

Design modern interfaces.

Keep layouts clean.

Maintain consistent spacing.

Support

Desktop

Tablet

Phone

Avoid clutter.

---

## Animations

Animations should feel smooth.

Prefer subtle movement.

Use easing.

Avoid flashy effects.

Performance is more important than complexity.

---

## Cinematics

Whenever major milestones occur

consider adding

camera movement

lighting

crowds

weather

music

sound

transitions

background motion

Examples

Signing with a label

Album release

Tour intro

Festival entrance

Award show

Hall of Fame

---

## Tour Intro Standard

When a player begins a concert

Show

A nighttime exterior of the venue.

The marquee displays

Artist name

Venue

City

Date

The marquee bulbs animate.

Street lights glow.

Traffic moves.

Fans gather outside.

Music slowly fades in.

Then transition into gameplay.

This should feel similar to classic American theaters.

Never use copyrighted imagery.

Create reusable systems.

---

## Polish

Whenever implementing UI

ask

"Would this feel satisfying to watch?"

If not

improve presentation before coding.
